# Yodacon

**The crew of shipmates on the Yodacon.**

In an Alaskan middle school with more children than classrooms, the overflow kids
were taught in portables parked in the snow. The building called them *the Outsiders*.
The next year they were told to rename their own home rooms, and the contest ran a
week. One of us had Infini-D and rendered an actual starship: the **Yodacon**.

The teachers' vote made it **Taurus**. The Yodacons lost.

But the ship got built. This organization gives it a purpose — reviving the
**ConEx** *Escape Velocity* plugin (Paul Richeson, 1999) where the Yodacon was
published, and expanding on the original game as **Team Yodacon**: commodity
trading with a landing sequence driven by real reentry-heating physics.
