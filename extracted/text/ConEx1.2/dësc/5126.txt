The key to making profit on the commodity exchange is buying low and selling high. In the Commodity Exchange window, this information is given next to the prices of items. In this mission, you will start at <DST> in the <DSY> system. Check <DST>'s Commodity Exchange and find out what goods they trade. Then, select adjecent systems on your map to find out what goods they trade, if any. Find adjecent systems that have planets that trade the same goods as <DST>, and check the prices. Buy a specific commodity low on one planet, then sell it high on another. Buy as much as you can low, and sell it all high. You have 60 days to complete this mission, but you may terminate it at any time by returning to <RST>. Do not abort the mission to end it, and make sure you return to <RST> before <DL>. Landing on <RST> will terminate the mission. Remember to hail the Overseer if you need any assistance.
