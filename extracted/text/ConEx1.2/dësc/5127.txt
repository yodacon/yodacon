This mission will be similar to that last. You must fly to <DST>, land, and check the commodity exchange. The Academy has rigged a disaster, changing the price of one of the commodities. Take advantage of this change by finding another world that sells that commodity at a lower price. You should also have enough money to purchase an escort for this mission. After you have finished reading this message, select "Hire Escort" from the Spaceport Bar. Hire a Courier. The price at the bottom is the ship's daily charge. The Academy has given you enough money to sustain this escort for a period of time. When you are ready to finish the mission, release the escort by targeting him (by pressing opt-tab until the ship is selected), hailing him ("y"), and selecting "Release from Servitude." Then, return to Levo to complete the mission. The time limit is 60 days, byt you may terminate earlier by landing on Levo.
