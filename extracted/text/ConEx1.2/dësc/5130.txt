Your first assignment is to destroy an Academy ship. Its weapons will do you no damage and its shields are not at full power. It will be attacking you as well, so try not to be hit by its weapons. Your ship is very maneuverable, so try not to overshoot the enemy or overmaneuver the ship. Your laser cannon fires straight in the direction of your ship. Try to make sure you can hit the enemy without hitting anyone else before firing. You should also make use of the new items your ship has. To detect where your enemy is, find the red dot on your radar screen. Larger dots indicate bigger ships. Blue dots indicate friendly ships, and green ones indicate escorts. Use your afterburner if you need to outrun or catch up with your enemy quicky. If your ship begins to explode, your auto-ejection system will launch your escape pod. Remember, if you need help, hail the Overseer and select "Greetings" for advice, and "Request Assistance" for help. Good luck.
