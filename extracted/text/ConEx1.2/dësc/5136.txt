This mission will require you to destroy three ConEx target ships that are minimally armed. These ships are not as difficult as real ships you will encounter in space, but their weapons can do you damage. Remember to use your secondary weapons and to watch your shields. Hail the Overseer for help, or land on  ConEx if you feel you will be destroyed. If you are, you will be safely ejected, but you will need to purchase another ship with the left-over money. If you have not added more torpedos or rockets to your ship, do so before leaving ConEx. Good luck.
