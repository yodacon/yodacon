Escorting ships involves taking them safely to their destination. Sometimes, you may need to destroy attacking ships or simply jump out of a system quickly. This mission will require you to escort three Light Freighters to <RST>. Their dots on your radar screen will be large and green. Two of our Academy ships will be following you, trying to destroy the Light Freighters. Either destroy the persuing Academy ships or jump between systems before they can destroy the Freighters. You may land to refuel between jumps. This action also recharges the Freighters' shields, so it may be advisable if one or more of them are damaged. If any of the ships are destroyed, the mission will fail. To complete the mission, land on <RST>. Good luck.
